THE 15-POINT METHOD

A website can look fine — and still quietly lose customers.

Most owners don't realize that minor technical and content friction points make visitors bounce to competitors.

🔗

Broken Links

Dead menus, 404 pages, or unclickable buttons stop customers right when they are ready to order.

📞

Missing Click-to-Call

If a mobile visitor cannot tap your phone number to dial in 1 click, 60% will hit 'back' to Google.

⏰

Outdated Details

Old holiday hours or an outdated copyright notice makes customers assume the business is inactive.

🛡️

Missing Trust Signals

Without Google reviews, SSL encryption, and clear guarantees, visitors hesitate to submit inquiries.
